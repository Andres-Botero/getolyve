Service providers can tap the search icon in the top-right corner of Home to search across their business. Client accounts currently have only partial search results, so clients should open visits and documents from Calendar or Inbox instead.

## Find something

1. Type a client name, address, document number, service, or other detail you remember.
2. Leave **All** selected to search everything, or choose **Contacts**, **Invoices**, **Estimates**, or **Payments**.
3. Tap a result to open it.

Results are grouped by type. A contact opens the client page. Invoices, estimates, and payments open their detail screens.

## Search tips

- Invoice and estimate searches can match a client, address, service, or document number.
- Payment searches match the payment number or client name, not the amount.
- **All** shows up to eight results from each type. Choose one filter to see more results in that category.
- Olyve starts searching after you pause typing for a moment.

If nothing appears, try fewer words or search only one category.
