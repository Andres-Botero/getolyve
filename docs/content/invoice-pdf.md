Every invoice created on Professional or Business includes a PDF.

## Preview

Open the invoice and tap **Preview**. Olyve prepares the document and displays it in the app. If it cannot load, tap **Retry**.

## Download

Tap **Download PDF**. Your phone opens its share sheet, where you can:

- Save the PDF to Files.
- Send it through Mail, Messages, or another app.
- Print it.

The filename begins with `Invoice_` followed by the invoice number, such as `Invoice_INV-12-2026-004.pdf`.

You can also open **View Invoice** from a calendar visit and tap **Download PDF** without leaving the day.

## What the PDF is

The document includes your saved business details, the client, service or line items, subtotal, tax, total, status, and notes.

This is the invoice you send your customer. Receipts for your own Olyve subscription are under **Billing & Plan → Billing history**.
