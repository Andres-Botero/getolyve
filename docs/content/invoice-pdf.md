Every invoice on Professional and Business has a PDF. Starter does not include invoice PDFs.

## Preview

Open the invoice and choose **Preview**. Olyve downloads the PDF and shows it in the app. If the file fails to load, the screen shows the error and a way to try again.

## Download

On the preview, **Download PDF** saves a file named `Invoice_` plus the invoice number, for example `Invoice_1042.pdf`. You need to be signed in. On a phone, the system share sheet lets you save it to Files, send it in Mail or Messages, or print it. That is the copy you hand to a client who is not in Olyve.

The same download is available from a visit’s invoice list on the calendar, so you do not have to leave the day to get the file.

## What the PDF is

The PDF is the issued invoice: your business details from [My account](/docs/my-account), the client, the service, the amounts, and the invoice number. It is not the Axial Motion receipt for your Olyve subscription. Those receipts are in Billing history.
