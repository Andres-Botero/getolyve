Olyve works best when you set up the basics in order: your business, your services, then your clients and their schedules.

## Create your provider account

1. On the sign-in screen, tap **Sign Up**.
2. Enter your name, email, and a password of at least six characters.
3. Add your phone and business name if you have them handy.
4. Tap **Create Account**.
5. After the success message, return to **Login** and sign in.

Your 14-day Professional trial starts without a card.

> Joining a service provider as their customer? Do not create a provider account. Tap **Join here** under **Have an invite code?** and follow [Join Olyve as a client](/docs/join-as-a-client).

## Add your business details

From Home, tap your photo, then **My Information**. Add the business name and address you want clients to see. These details are also used on invoice PDFs.

Tap **Save** before leaving the screen.

## Create your first service

1. Return to Home and open **My Services**.
2. Tap **Add**.
3. Name the service and set a default price.
4. Tap **Save**.

You can override the default price for any client. See [Create and price services](/docs/services).

## Add a client and schedule work

1. Open **My Clients** and tap **Add**.
2. Choose **Add one**, enter the client, and tap **Save**.
3. Open the client and tap **+ Add Service**.
4. Choose your service and confirm the price.
5. Tap **Schedule** beside that service.
6. Choose a one-time or recurring schedule and save it.

The visit now appears on your calendar.

## Finish the first visit

On the day of the job, open **Calendar** and tap **Mark Complete**. Add a note about the work if useful.

After completion, create an invoice or record a direct payment. Olyve keeps the visit, invoice, and payment connected so you can find the full story later.
