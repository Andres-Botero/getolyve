Open **My Payments** from Home for a record of money you have already received. New payments are recorded from an invoice or completed visit, not from this list.

## The list

Each row shows the payment number, date, amount, client, method, notes, and whether it is **From Invoice** or a **Direct Payment**. Tap **View payment** to open the full record and its related invoice or visit.

## Find one

Search by payment number or client name. Amounts are not part of the payment search. Use **Start Date** and **End Date** to filter by payment date, and **Clear** to remove the range.

Olyve shows 50 payments at a time. Use **Prev** and **Next** to move between pages.

The search icon on Home can also find payments by number or client and open the same detail screen.

There is no **+ New** on this screen. A payment always starts from the work it pays for, so the amount stays attached to an invoice or a visit.
