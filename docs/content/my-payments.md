**My Payments** is the Home card for money you have already recorded. It is the ledger, not the place you create a charge. Record a new one from an invoice or a completed visit. See [Payments](/docs/payments).

## The list

Each row shows the amount, the method (cash, check, Zelle, or bank transfer), and whether it is **From Invoice** or a **Direct Payment**. **View payment** opens the detail: number, client, date, amount, method, notes, and the invoice or visit it belongs to.

## Find one

Search the list, or set a start date and end date. **Clear** removes the dates. An empty filter says to adjust the search. A brand-new account says payments will appear here once recorded.

The list is paged with **Prev** and **Next**.

Header search on Home also finds payments by client or amount and opens the same detail screen.

There is no **+ New** on this screen. A payment always starts from the work it pays for, so the amount stays attached to an invoice or a visit.
