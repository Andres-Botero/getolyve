**My Invoices** is the Home card for every bill you have issued. It is included with Professional and Business.

## The list

Each row shows paid or unpaid, the total, the client, the service, and the service date. If a balance is left, the row shows **Remaining**. **Mark as Paid** is on the row for anything still open. **View invoice** opens the document. See [Invoices](/docs/invoices) and [Payments](/docs/payments).

## Find one

Search by the text you remember: client, number, or service. Set a **Start Date** and **End Date**, then **Clear** to drop the range. If nothing matches, the screen says to adjust the search or filters. If you have never issued one, it says invoices will appear here once generated, with a **Create Invoice** button.

The list is paged. Use **Prev** and **Next** under the rows.

## Create one here

**+ New** starts an invoice that is not opened from a visit. For work already on the calendar, create the invoice from the completed visit so the price and the stop stay tied together.

Search from the Home header also finds invoices and jumps you to the same detail screen.
