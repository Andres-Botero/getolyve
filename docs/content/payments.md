Olyve records the payments your customers make to you. It does not charge their card. Card checkout in the app is only for your own Olyve subscription. See [Billing and plans](/docs/billing).

The full list is [My payments](/docs/my-payments). This page is how a payment gets recorded.

## Ways to record one

**On an unpaid invoice.** Open the invoice, or use **Mark as Paid** on My Invoices or on the visit. Olyve loads what is still due. Enter the amount, the date, and the method.

**On a completed visit with no invoice.** **Record Payment** on the visit writes a direct payment against that stop. Use this when you collected money without issuing a bill. Starter plans can record these payments even though they cannot create invoices.

You cannot record either kind while the visit is still scheduled.

## Methods

- Cash
- Check
- Zelle
- Bank transfer

Add a note if you need to remember a check number or who handed you the cash. That note shows on the payment and in visit history.

## What changes after you save

The payment appears in My Payments, labeled **From Invoice** or **Direct Payment**. An invoice payment reduces the remaining balance. When the balance hits zero, the invoice becomes Paid and the visit display status becomes Paid. Status edits on that visit lock.

Open the payment for its number, amount, method, date, client, and the invoice or visit it belongs to.
