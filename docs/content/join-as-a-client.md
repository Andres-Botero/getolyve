Your service provider sends you an invite code when they add you to Olyve. Client accounts are free—you do not need to choose a plan or enter a card.

## Join with your code

1. Open Olyve and go to the sign-in screen.
2. Under **Have an invite code?**, tap **Join here**.
3. Enter the code your provider sent and tap **Continue**.
4. Check the business and client name shown on screen.
5. Enter your email, full name, and a password of at least six characters.
6. Tap **Join**.

Olyve signs you in and adds that provider to your account.

## If you already have an Olyve account

Enter the email and password for your existing account. Accepting another invite adds the company to your calendar instead of creating a second account.

Tap **Use a different code** if the business or client name does not look right. An invalid or expired code shows **Invite not found**; ask your provider for a new invitation.

## What you can do

From Home, use:

- **My Schedule** on Home to see upcoming and past visits. It opens the **Calendar** tab, where the screen title is **My Calendar**.
- **Inbox** for visit, invoice, and payment updates.
- **Inbox → Messages** to write your service provider.

Open visits from My Schedule or Calendar. Invoices and payments open from Inbox notifications or from a related visit; client Home does not have separate My Invoices or My Payments sections. Your provider remains responsible for scheduling work, changing prices, creating invoices, and recording payments.
