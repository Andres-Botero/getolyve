An invoice is the bill for work. Professional and Business include invoices and PDF copies. Starter does not. The list of every invoice is [My invoices](/docs/my-invoices). This page is how one invoice comes to exist and what is on it.

## From a completed visit

This is the normal path. On the visit, **Create Invoice** loads a preview: the client, the service, the visit date, and the visit price as the subtotal. Confirm it and Olyve issues the invoice. The visit then shows as invoiced, and the action changes to **View Invoice**. You cannot create a second invoice for a visit that already has one.

You cannot invoice a visit that is still scheduled.

## From My Invoices

**+ New** on My Invoices starts a blank invoice when the bill is not tied to a single visit button. Pick the client by search. Olyve fills name, address, email, and phone from the client record. Add the service and amounts, then save. The new invoice shows up in the list as unpaid.

## What an invoice holds

Open any invoice for three tabs:

- **Summary** — client, service, service date, totals, and paid or unpaid. If a balance remains, the provider sees **Mark as Paid**.
- **Preview** — the PDF. See [Downloadable PDF invoice](/docs/invoice-pdf).
- **History** — when it was issued and each payment applied to it.

A client who has joined Olyve can open an invoice you sent them. They can read the summary, preview, and history. They do not get **Mark as Paid**. You record the payment on your side.

## Paid and unpaid

An invoice is **Unpaid** until the recorded payments cover the total. A partial payment leaves a remaining balance, shown on the list. **Paid** means the balance is zero. Marking it paid is covered in [Payments](/docs/payments).
