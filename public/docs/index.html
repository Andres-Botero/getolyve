User guide

How Olyve is organized, and where to go for each part of the job.

Olyve is the book for a home-service week: who you serve, when you go, what you did, and whether it has been billed. This guide follows the app the way it is laid out on Home.

Account

My accountProfile photo, business details, review links, and where account tools live. SettingsDark mode, English or Spanish, and the shortcut to billing. Billing and plansThe trial, Starter, Professional, and Business, plus cards and receipts.

Communication

InboxNotifications and messages in one place, with an unread count on Home. ChatStart a thread, reply, and message a client who has joined Olyve.

The work

Client managementAdd people or companies, import a list, invite them, and attach services. My servicesYour price list — name, description, and a default price you can override per client. CalendarThe week of visits, plus reschedule, cancel, complete, and reopen from the day. VisitsWhat a visit record holds, and the path from scheduled to paid. Visit actionsComplete, invoice, record a payment, edit the price, request a review, or change status. Visit historyThe timeline on a visit — created, completed, invoiced, and paid.

Money

InvoicesHow an invoice is created from a visit or from scratch, and what the document contains. PaymentsRecord cash, check, Zelle, or a bank transfer against an invoice or a visit. Downloadable PDF invoicePreview the invoice PDF in the app and save Invoice_number.pdf. My invoicesSearch, filter by date, and page through every invoice you have issued. My paymentsEvery payment you have recorded, with method, source, and a date filter. My reportsCollected, invoiced, what is still open, and who to follow up with. My estimatesQuote one-time or recurring work, with tax and a valid-until date.

Growth

How the app is organized

The profile photo in the top left opens My Account. Search in the top right looks across clients, invoices, estimates, and payments.

The bottom tabs are Home, Calendar, and Clients. Services stay one tap away from Home, under My Services.

On Home, a service provider sees three groups of cards:

  • Work: Inbox, My Clients, My Services, My Calendar
  • Money: My Invoices, My Payments, My Reports, My Estimates
  • Growth: Request a Review

A client who joins with an invite sees Inbox and My Schedule. Client accounts are free. You pay for Olyve; your customers pay you for the work.

A visit is the center

The usual path is: add a client, attach a service, put a visit on the calendar, mark it complete, then invoice it or record the payment. After a visit is invoiced, the action changes from Create Invoice to View Invoice. After it is paid, the visit status stays locked.

Plans

New provider accounts start on a 14-day Professional trial. Starter covers a solo route. Professional adds invoices, estimates, PDFs, the client portal, messaging, and reports. Business adds priority support. See Billing and plans.

Language

The app is in English or Spanish. Change it in Settings. This guide is in English.
