For home-service operators

Tuesday has four stops.

One still needs an invoice. That’s the kind of day Olyve is for. Clients, the calendar, and the bill from the visit — not a notebook and a promise to invoice Friday.

Coming soon App Store Coming soon Google Play

The day

Put the visit on the calendar. Bill it from there.

Schedule a service. Mark it done. From a completed visit you generate the invoice or record the payment — the amount comes from that visit, not a blank form. After it’s billed, it’s View Invoice.

The book

Clients, then the services you do for them.

People or companies, on an A–Z list you can search. Add one by hand, or import from contacts, a file, or a paste. Services are your price list. Attach them to a client before you put a visit on the week.

The money

Quote it, bill the visit, see what’s still open.

An estimate can be one-time, weekly, biweekly, or monthly. Invoices and payments sit with the work. Reports for this week or this month: completed visits you haven’t invoiced yet, what’s outstanding, and how that compares to last time.

Inbox

The thread sits next to the job.

Notifications in one tab, messages in the other. If a client uses Olyve, they see their schedule and can write you there. That’s the whole idea.

Who it’s for

If the week is a list of addresses, this is for you.

Landscaping, pest, pool, cleaning, and shops that look like them. If you still keep the book in your head, you’re who we built it for.
