Delete your Olyve account

Google Play requires a publicly reachable way to request account deletion for apps that create accounts. Apple requires deletion to be available in the app as well. This page is the web path.

How to request deletion

  1. Email andres@axialmotion.com from the same address you use to sign in.
  2. Use the subject line “Delete my Olyve account”.
  3. Tell us if you also need an export of invoices or client records before we delete.

We will confirm the request, delete the account and personal data we are not required to keep, and email you when it is finished. That usually happens within 30 days.

What is removed

  • Your login and profile.
  • Clients, schedules, messages, estimates, and invoices stored for that account, except records we must keep for tax or legal reasons.
  • Client invitations and memberships tied to that account.

What may remain

We may retain limited records if the law requires it (for example invoice history) or if we need them to resolve a dispute or prevent abuse. Those copies are not used to provide the product.
