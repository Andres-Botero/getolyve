Billing

What you pay for

Effective September 22, 2026.

Olyve is operations software for service businesses: clients, visits, estimates, invoices, and inbox. Charges on this site are for Olyve usage — a subscription or a usage-based fee, as shown before you pay.

Olyve is a product of Axial Motion. Axial Motion is the company that operates the app, receives the payment, and holds the bank account. The name on your card or bank statement will include Axial Motion.

Currency and prices

Prices are in United States dollars (USD). The amount you will pay is shown before you confirm. Private beta may be free. When a paid plan is offered, it is labeled at checkout or in the app store listing. We are not running a promotion, trial discount, or coupon on this page.

How you pay

Web and in-app card payments are processed by Stripe on behalf of Axial Motion. We do not store full card numbers. Payment data is handled under Stripe’s PCI DSS compliance. This website and checkout use HTTPS.

If you buy through the Apple App Store or Google Play, Apple or Google charges you. Those purchases follow the store’s billing rules.

Cards commonly accepted through Stripe include:

  • Visa
  • Mastercard
  • American Express
  • Discover

Delivery

Olyve is digital software. After a successful payment, access is delivered online — usually immediately — to the account you used. There is no physical shipment, no shipping fee, and no delivery address.

Who can buy

Olyve is for businesses. You must be old enough to form a contract where you live. We do not sell to people or organizations we are prohibited from serving under United States law, including applicable sanctions.
